The Problem

Beauty is a $700B industry built on guesswork.

Consumers struggle to navigate thousands of products without clear guidance, while brands invest heavily in sampling yet incorrect product matching remains widespread.

54,000+ Wearable Products
60% Mismatch Rate
65 Foundation Shades
50% Max Return Rate

Four Core Issues

Why beauty shopping is broken.

01

Decision Fatigue

Consumers face an overwhelming choice when selecting the best beauty product for themselves. With 54,000+ wearable products on the market and new launches every two weeks, the sheer volume creates paralysis. Most people give up and guess — and guess wrong.

02

Overwhelming Number of Products

Every two weeks, many new products launch and consumers are often completely unaware. The market moves faster than any individual can track. Brand shade finders are siloed, inconsistent, and only work for their own catalogue — leaving shoppers stranded when comparing across brands.

03

Trend-Driven, Biased Discovery

The main discovery method today is influencers and advertising, which relies on social media algorithms and biased reviews. What gets promoted isn't what matches you — it's what was paid to be seen. Consumers deserve objective, personalised recommendations, not sponsored content.

04

Inefficient Sampling & High Return Costs

Beauty brands spend heavily on sampling — a ~$1.2B market — with campaigns costing up to $100K+ each. Despite this investment, up to 50% of samples go unused, and incorrect shade matching continues to drive high return rates of 20–50%, burning money for both brands and consumers.

The result? Wasted money, frustrated shoppers, and an industry that spends billions on a broken system.
